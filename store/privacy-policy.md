# RadioSpark — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-03 · Publisher: SparkLab (sparklab.bg)_

RadioSpark is an internet radio player for Android phones, Google TV, Android Auto, iPhone, iPad and Mac.

**We do not collect, store or sell personal data.** RadioSpark has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking SDKs.

- **Stored on your device only:** favorites, stations you add, the last station played and app settings. Uninstalling the app removes them.
- **Network requests:** to show the station list the app queries the public Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info). To play audio, your device connects directly to the station's streaming server you choose. These third parties receive your IP address as part of any normal internet connection and are governed by their own policies.
- **Children:** the app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
