RadioSpark brings thousands of live radio stations to your phone, your TV and your car.

• Hundreds of Bulgarian stations plus Greek and international favorites
• Browse by country or genre: Pop, Rock, Jazz, Classical, News/Talk, Electronic, Chill and more
• Favorites you can reorder, and your own custom stream URLs
• Live song titles when the station provides them
• Android Auto: browse Favorites, Countries and Genres safely from your car display, and resume your last station automatically
• Google TV: a 10-foot interface built for the remote control
• Dark Material design, lock-screen and notification controls
• No account, no ads, no tracking

Station directory powered by the free Radio Browser community project.
